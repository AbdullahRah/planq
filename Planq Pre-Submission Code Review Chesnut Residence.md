## Finding details

### F1. Rooftop guard height (Fail)

**Requirement.** 9.8.8.3.(1): guards shall be not less than 1 070 mm high. The 900 mm exception in 9.8.8.3.(3) applies only where the walking surface is not more than 1 800 mm above finished ground.

**Drawings show.** The roof plan shows a stair to the rooftop. Section A gives the rooftop at +5.85 and the parapet top at +6.35, a guard height of 0.50 m.

**Fix.** Provide a guard of at least 1 070 mm around the accessible roof, with openings that stop a 100 mm sphere (9.8.8.5.).

### F2. Door widths at entrance hall and stair (Needs confirmation)

**Requirement.** Table 9.5.5.1.: 810 mm minimum width at the required entrance, a vestibule or entrance hall, and stairs to a floor with finished space.

**Drawings show.** The main entrance is tagged P1 (1.10 m), which complies. Several ground floor doors are tagged P2 (0.80 m). Locations near the entrance hall and stair cannot be confirmed from the tags.

**Fix.** Confirm which P2 doors serve the entrance hall or the stair and widen those to 810 mm.

### F3. Riser count conflict (Drawing conflict)

**Requirement.** 9.8.4.1., Table 9.8.4.1.: private stair rise between 125 mm and 200 mm.

**Drawings show.** The ground floor plan numbers 16 risers; Section B numbers 17. Over a 2.85 m floor-to-floor rise this gives 178 mm or 168 mm. Both comply, but the sheets disagree.

**Fix.** Correct the plan or the section so the riser count and dimensions match.

### F4. Side walls at property lines (Can't determine)

**Requirement.** 9.10.15.5.(2): where limiting distance is less than 0.6 m, the exposing building face needs a fire-resistance rating of at least 45 min and metal or noncombustible cladding, or one of the other listed options. 9.10.15.4. limits glazed openings.

**Drawings show.** Building width 9.83 m on a 10.00 m lot; Sheet 02 marks the property limit at both side walls. No wall assembly, rating or cladding is given.

**Fix.** Specify the side wall assembly, its rating, cladding, and confirm no glazed openings beyond the Table 9.10.15.4. limits.

### F5. Bedroom egress windows (Can't determine)

**Requirement.** 9.9.10.1.: unless sprinklered, each bedroom needs an openable window or exterior door; the window opening must be at least 0.35 m² with no dimension less than 380 mm.

**Drawings show.** Bedrooms A and B, the principal bedroom and the maid's room have no window sizes or operation shown.

**Fix.** Add a window schedule giving clear openable dimensions for each bedroom window.

### F6. Attached garage separation (Can't determine)

**Requirement.** 9.10.9.18.(4): an air barrier system between the garage and the house. 9.10.13.15.: the connecting door must be tight fitting, weather-stripped and self-closing, and must not open into a sleeping room.

**Drawings show.** The two-car garage connects to the house interior. No air barrier or door specification is shown.

**Fix.** Note the air barrier on the garage walls and ceiling and specify the connecting door.

### F7. Smoke and CO alarms (Can't determine)

**Requirement.** 9.10.19.1. and 9.10.19.3.: smoke alarms on each storey, in each sleeping room and between sleeping rooms and the rest of the storey. 9.32.3.9.: CO alarms where the building contains a storage garage or a fuel-burning appliance.

**Drawings show.** No alarms on any sheet.

**Fix.** Show smoke and CO alarm locations on the floor plans or an electrical sheet.

### F8. Energy efficiency (Can't determine)

**Requirement.** Section 9.36., with Tier 1 as the Alberta minimum.

**Drawings show.** No insulation values, window performance, airtightness target or compliance path.

**Fix.** State the compliance path (prescriptive, trade-off or performance) and provide assembly details.
